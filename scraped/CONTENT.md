**Road to Design:** Road to D-School is an Educational program from Lomoni Design studio for school and college students who want to take design as a career, offering students a series of mentoring, guidance, and Practice with fellow Designers.

**What We Do:** We focus only on three design entrance exams, NID, NIFT, and CEED/UCEED, for bachelor's and master's students. Services include individual training, workshops, skill development, and portfolio building. Mentors are designers from NID and NIFT.

### FAQ
- **What is a Design Entrance?** Design entrance exams are different from any other entrance exam. These exams are focused on testing a student's creativity, Logical thinking, sketching, and problem-solving skills.
- **I don't have good drawing skills, will I be able to attend?** Basic sketching skills are enough to take this exam.
- **What is Portfolio?** Your portfolio is a collection of your work that shows your design ideas, skillset, knowledge and, most importantly, your approach and process.

## R-2-D | Road To D-School

We are not just a coaching institute. It is a mentoring and career guidance initiative run by Lomoni Design Studio and targeted at bridging the gap between Student and Design Education and Design careers.

Road To D-School targets three design entrance exams: NID, NIFT, and CEED/UCEED for bachelor's and master's students.

**Program areas:**
- **Creative Ability & Design Aptitude** — visual/spatial ability, environmental and social awareness, design elements/principles, color theory, general knowledge.
- **Skill Training in Drawing** — fundamentals, shading, rendering, perspective, anatomy, composition, optical illusion.
- **Skill Training in Design** — logo design, poster design, product design, storyboarding, animation, interaction design basics.
- **Workshops** — design tools (Illustrator, Photoshop, Figma), material manipulation, model making, photography, portfolio creation, interview skills.

## COURSES

### NID — Bachelor's (B.Des)
NID is internationally acclaimed as one of the finest educational and research institutions for Industrial, Communication, Textile, and IT Integrated (Experiential) Design; recognized as an Institution of National Importance.
- **Exam:** NID Design Aptitude Test (NID DAT)
- **Eligibility:** Passed/appearing class 12 in any discipline (Science, Arts, Commerce, Humanities) from any recognized board (CBSE, IB, ICSE or equivalent).
- **Pattern:** 3 stages — Preliminary Test, Studio Test, Interview.
- **Programs (Ahmedabad):** Communication Design (Animation Film, Exhibition, Film & Video Communication, Graphic), Industrial Design (Ceramic & Glass, Product, Furniture), Textile/Apparel/Lifestyle/Accessory Design.
- **Timeline:** Applications October; written exam January; results February; final exams March–May; results May.

### UCEED — IITs (B.Des)
Undergraduate Common Entrance Exam for Design, conducted by IIT Bombay; for B.Des at 5 IITs.
- **Eligibility:** Passed Class XII (or equivalent) previous year, or appearing current year, ANY STREAM.
- **Pattern:** Single stage, 3 hours. Part A computer-based; Part B sketching.
- **Institutions:** IIT Bombay (IDC), IIT Delhi, IIT Guwahati, IIT Hyderabad, IIITDM Jabalpur.
- **Timeline:** Applications Sep/Oct; admit cards January; exam late Jan–Feb; results April.
- Note: UCEED score also accepted by several private institutes.

### NIFT — Bachelor's (B.Des / B.F.Tech)
NIFT established 1986 under the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India.
- **Age:** < 24 as of 1st August of admission year (5-yr relaxation SC/ST/PWD).
- **Eligibility:** 10+2 from recognized board; B.F.Tech also requires Physics & Mathematics.
- **Pattern:** B.Des → CAT (Creative Ability Test) + GAT (General Ability Test). B.F.Tech → GAT only. Negative marking 25% per wrong answer.
- **B.Des:** Fashion Design, Leather Design, Accessory Design, Textile Design, Knitwear Design, Fashion Communication. **B.F.Tech:** Apparel Production.
- **Timeline:** Applications January; exam early February; results March; finals May; counseling May–June.
- **17 campuses** across India.

### NID — Master's (M.Des)
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's degree, OR 4-year full-time diploma in design/fine arts/applied arts/architecture.
- **Exam:** NID DAT — 3 stages: Preliminary Test, Studio Test, Interview.
- **Campuses:** Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar, Bangalore.
- **Programs:** 5 faculty streams across 19 design domains.
- **Timeline:** Applications October; written January; results February; studio + interview May; final results May.

### CEED — IITs (M.Des)
Conducted by IIT Bombay for Master's programs across eight IITs.
- **Eligibility:** Degree/diploma/PG of min. 3 years (after 10+2) or currently enrolled. No age limit; multiple attempts allowed.
- **Pattern:** 3 hours. Part A (1 hr, 100 marks, NAT/MCQ/MSQ). Part B (2 hr, 5 questions: sketching, creativity, form/visual sensitivity, problem identification).
- **Institutions:** IDC IIT Bombay (Industrial, Communication, Animation, Interaction, Mobility Design), IISc Bangalore, IIT Delhi/Guwahati/Hyderabad/Kanpur, IIITDM Jabalpur.
- **Timeline:** Applications Sep/Oct; exam late Jan–Feb; results March.

### NIFT — Master's (M.Des / M.F.M / M.F.Tech)
- **Age:** No limit.
- **Eligibility:** M.Des/M.F.M → UG degree in any discipline or 3-yr NIFT/NID diploma. M.F.Tech → B.F.Tech (NIFT) or B.E/B.Tech.
- **Pattern:** Written (CAT+GAT for M.Des; GAT for others) → Group Discussion + Personal Interview. Negative marking 25%.
- **Programs:** M.Des, M.F.M, M.F.Tech.
- **Timeline:** Applications January; exam early Feb; results March; interviews April; final May; counseling May–June.
